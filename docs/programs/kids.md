# LESSGOOO Kids

Status: CONFIRMED PROGRAM FAMILY

## Purpose

Practical computer and technology education for children with an emphasis on
continuous learning rather than a single holiday course.

Learning may include:
- computer fundamentals;
- basic digital literacy;
- AI awareness/use appropriate to learners;
- programming foundations;
- IoT/Arduino demonstrations and projects;
- practical technology activities.

Weekend continuity classes have been an important LESSGOOO direction.

Exact ages, current schedule, fees and cohort dates must be confirmed before
publication.
