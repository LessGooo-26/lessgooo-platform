## Decision

Institutional and business facts used by the application must originate from
the repository knowledge base and/or approved structured content.

Codex must not infer missing official facts from generic school software
patterns.

## Consequences

Unknown information is tracked in `docs/UNKNOWN.md`.

Confirmed business changes update documentation before or together with code.
